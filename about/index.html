About Cincinnati School of Music

A school built by teachers, for teachers.

Our Story

From Two Teachers to Five Locations

Cincinnati School of Music began long before we had multiple schools or a team of 60 instructors. In 2010, we started as Backer Piano Studio, just the two of us driving from home to home across Cincinnati to give lessons. Every new student felt like a gift, a chance to learn how to teach better, connect deeper, and motivate students to love their instrument.

Each parent conversation, scheduling challenge, and student breakthrough taught us how to build systems that balanced organization, consistency, and fairness while keeping families happy and supported.

By 2012, we were ready for a home of our own. We opened our first brick-and-mortar location in a small Montgomery basement, hired a few teachers, and quickly discovered we had tapped into something special. Word of mouth spread fast, so fast that within nine months, we had outgrown the space and moved across the street to a larger Montgomery studio. That became our home for the next nine years.

During that time, we expanded into Mason (2016) and Anderson (2017), growing steadily through the trust of teachers, students, and families who believed in what we were building. In 2022, we moved our Montgomery school once more, this time to a beautiful new space designed for the community we had grown.

And in 2025, we opened our newest locations in Maineville and Middletown, completing a journey that began fifteen years earlier with two teachers, a handful of students, and a car full of music books.

What Makes CSM Different

Built Around Teachers

Most teachers have worked somewhere that left them juggling too much, tracking payments, chasing students, answering parents, or watching their schedule fall apart. We decided to build the opposite.

At CSM, our admin team handles all the communication, scheduling, billing, and recital logistics. You get a clean schedule, steady pay, and the time to actually teach. That is why so many of our teachers stay with us for years. They can finally focus on what they are good at.

Our Values

What We Stand For

  • Respect for your craft. We hire talented teachers and treat them as professionals, because they are.
  • Organization over chaos. Reliable systems keep our days smooth and predictable.
  • Community that lasts. We invest in long-term relationships, growth, and shared success.

We know how much teachers give of themselves, and how much smoother teaching feels when the right systems are in place. The result is a steady schedule, dependable income, and a community that values professionalism and respect.
